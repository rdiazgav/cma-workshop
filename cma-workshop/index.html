Custom Metrics Autoscaler on OpenShift Local

Scale on what actually matters

The Custom Metrics Autoscaler (CMA) extends OpenShift horizontal pod autoscaling beyond CPU and memory. This workshop runs four progressive steps on a single OpenShift Local (CRC) cluster, starting from a fixed-replica deployment and adding CPU, Prometheus, and cron-based scaling triggers.
